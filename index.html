Frequently asked questions

Which areas of Dubai do you cover?

Dubai Marina, Palm Jumeirah, Jebel Ali, Al Satwa, Downtown Dubai, Business Bay, Jumeirah 1, Jumeirah 2, Arabian Ranches, The Meadows, The Springs, Emirates Hills and JLT (Jumeirah Lake Towers). If you're nearby but not listed, call us — we'll do our best.

What services does Al Hamra offer?

Maintenance work of every kind: AC service & repair, electrical work, plumbing work, tile fixing, painting work, carpentry work, gypsum work, door & lock repair and general maintenance for homes, villas and offices.

How do I book a visit?

Call +971 50 262 2908 or +971 50 715 6512, message us on WhatsApp, email alhmratechnicalservices.info@gmail.com, or use the booking form on this page — it opens WhatsApp or your email app with your request ready to send.

Do you give a price before starting work?

Yes. Our technician inspects the problem first and explains the work and cost clearly, so you can approve it before any work begins. Final pricing depends on the job, materials and access — we never invent a one-price-fits-all figure.

My AC stopped cooling — what should I check first?

Check the thermostat setting, make sure the filter isn't clogged, and confirm the outdoor unit isn't blocked. If it still blows warm air or drips water, book an AC service — our guide “Why your AC isn't cooling” explains the common causes in Dubai homes.

Can you handle several jobs in one visit?

Often, yes — that's the advantage of one multi-trade team. Mention everything when you book (for example AC service plus a leaking tap plus a sticking door) and we'll plan the visit so the right specialists and materials come together.
