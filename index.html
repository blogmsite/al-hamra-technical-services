Frequently asked questions

Which areas of Dubai do you cover?

Dubai Marina, Palm Jumeirah, Jebel Ali, Al Satwa, Downtown, Business Bay, Jumeirah 1 and 2, Arabian Ranches, The Meadows, The Springs, Emirates Hills, JLT. That's most days. If you're nearby but not on that list, ring us anyway. We'd rather tell you yes than have you guess.

What services does Al Hamra offer?

Pretty much all of it, yes. AC, electrics, plumbing, tiling, painting, carpentry, gypsum, locks, general maintenance. Homes, villas, offices. If you're not sure whether it's us, ring and ask. Takes thirty seconds.

How do I book a visit?

Ring either number, WhatsApp us, email, or use the booking form on this page. The form opens WhatsApp or your email app with your request already written out. You just press send. Or ring. Ringing is still the fastest way.

Do you give a price before starting work?

Yes. Always. The technician looks first, tells you what needs doing and what it'll cost, and nothing starts until you say yes. Final price depends on the job, the materials, how easy it is to get to. No two jobs are the same, so no, we don't do one price for everything. Anyone who does is guessing.

My AC stopped cooling. What should I check first?

Start with the easy stuff. Thermostat on cool? Temperature low enough? Filter clean, not clogged with dust? Outdoor unit clear, nothing piled up against it? If you've checked all that and it's still blowing warm air, or dripping water, stop there and book a service. There's a guide on this page, “Why your AC isn't cooling”, that goes through the common causes in Dubai homes, but honestly, if the easy checks didn't fix it, it's a technician job now.

Can you handle several jobs in one visit?

Often, yes. And it's cheaper that way, one visit instead of three. Just tell us everything when you book. AC service, leaking tap, door that sticks, the lot. We'll bring the right people and the right parts, in one visit. Don't book three separate visits and pay for the driving three times.
