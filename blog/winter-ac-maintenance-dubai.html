Winter AC Maintenance in Dubai: Why November Is the Best Time to Service Your AC

Your air conditioner has just worked harder than it will all year. Six months of Dubai summer, dust, humidity and salt air are still sitting inside it. November is when you fix that — quietly, cheaply, and before the heat comes back.

By Al Hamra Technical Services · Published 9 November 2026 · Category: AC Maintenance · Dubai, UAE

Here is the honest version of winter AC maintenance in Dubai. Your AC did not get a break this summer. From May to October it ran most of the day, most days, in 40-degree heat, pulling in dust from the shamal winds and humidity off the coast. Then November arrives, the evenings cool down, and you switch it off — or barely use it — for the next few months. That quiet stretch is the best time to service it. Not because something is broken. Because nothing is broken yet, and you can keep it that way.

This guide covers what a winter service actually includes, what it costs in Dubai this year, how it helps your DEWA bill, and when to book — written by the team that does the work in Marina towers, Palm villas and JLT flats every week.

Why November is the best window for winter AC maintenance in Dubai

November to February is the recognised winter maintenance window in Dubai. The big guides all say the same thing: service the AC after summer, before summer. Fazal Technical's winter AC guide makes the case for the cold months, Octopus sets out how often a Dubai AC should be serviced, and the Geem calendar puts the quiet season to work — November for painting and renovation, December for water-heater servicing.

Three reasons November wins. First, the damage is fresh — dust in the coils, a drain starting to clog, a tired capacitor — and a technician can fix it now instead of discovering it in April when the unit gives up on the first hot weekend. Second, technicians are free: in summer every AC company in Dubai is booked solid, in November you pick a day that suits you. Third, you get the whole winter to test it. Run the AC on a cool evening after the service; if something still sounds wrong, there is time to sort it.

AC demand in Dubai drops 60–70% from November to March compared with summer, according to Clozer's 2026 HVAC marketing guide for Dubai. That is why maintenance is cheaper and easier to book now: the winter visit is planned work, not an emergency call-out. If you live in a Marina tower, a Palm villa or a JLT flat, book in November or December — leave it until March and you are queuing with everyone else.

What a Dubai summer does to your AC

Dubai summers are hard on air conditioners in ways you cannot see from a cool living room. The outdoor unit has breathed in fine dust for months, the filters have caught it, the drain line has carried condensation all summer, and near the coast — the Marina, Palm Jumeirah, Jumeirah — salt air adds slow wear to the outdoor unit.

It rarely stops the AC in one go. It shows up slowly: the room takes longer to cool, a musty smell appears on start-up, a drip shows under the indoor unit, the bill creeps up though you changed nothing. Each is a small sign, and each is cheaper to fix in November than in May.

Our own guide to why your AC isn't cooling in Dubai goes through the common causes one by one — clogged filters, blocked drains, low refrigerant, a tired compressor. Most of them start as summer wear. A winter service is how you catch them while they are still small jobs.

The signs your AC needs a winter visit

  • It cools more slowly than it did in spring, or one room is cooler than the rest.
  • There is a musty smell for the first few minutes after switch-on, or water drips from the indoor unit.
  • The outdoor unit sounds louder, or cycles on and off more than it used to.
  • Your DEWA bill stayed high even after the weather cooled, or it has been more than six months since the last service.

If two or more of those sound familiar, do not wait for summer to test the unit for you. That test costs more.

What a proper winter AC maintenance visit covers

A proper AC maintenance Dubai visit is not a five-minute spray and a wave. It is a checklist, and you should be able to see the technician working through it. This is what a winter visit from Al Hamra covers.

The winter visit checklist

  1. Filters out, cleaned or replaced. A clogged filter is the most common reason an AC cools badly — and the easiest fix in the whole visit.
  2. Coils cleaned. Fouled coils cannot move heat properly, so the compressor runs longer. Cleaning them is the biggest efficiency win in a service.
  3. Drain line cleared and tested. The condensate drain clogs slowly with dust and algae. Clear it, flush it, prove it drains — a blocked drain is how ceilings get stained.
  4. Outdoor unit checked. Fan, fins, mounting and wiring, plus a look for rust or salt-air damage on coastal buildings. Anything loose or corroded gets flagged before it fails.
  5. Refrigerant and electrical checks. Pressures checked, connections tightened, capacitor and contactor looked at. Low refrigerant is not topped up and forgotten — the leak is found first.
  6. Thermostat tested. Set it, run the unit, check the room reaches the temperature it promises. A thermostat that reads wrong wastes electricity all summer.
  7. Full run and clean-up. The unit runs, cools properly, and the area is left clean. The technician tells you what was done and what, if anything, needs watching.

For a normal split unit in a flat, that takes roughly 45 minutes to an hour; a villa with five or six units takes a morning. Anyone who promises a full service in ten minutes is not doing the checklist above. Our guide on AC servicing in Dubai — how often, and what a proper service includes sets out the intervals: every three to six months for most Dubai homes, at least once a year before summer.

How winter AC maintenance helps you reduce your DEWA bill

Let us be straight: a winter service will not halve your DEWA bill. But a dirty AC is one of the quietest ways a Dubai home wastes electricity, and cleaning it is one of the cheapest ways to stop it. If you want to know how to reduce DEWA bill in Dubai, start with the machine that uses the most power in your home.

A clogged filter or fouled coil forces the compressor to run longer to reach the same temperature, and DEWA charges in slabs — the more you use, the higher the rate on the extra units. MoneyCompare's DEWA bill explainer sets out the slab structure, and Bayut's guide puts AC efficiency at the top of the list. The second half is the thermostat: DEWA's guidance is 24°C, repeated in the Gulf News guide and Property Finder's guide. Every degree below 24°C costs noticeably more to hold — and a serviced AC reaches 24°C faster than a dirty one.

Three things that actually move a DEWA bill: clean the filters every month or two between services; keep the thermostat at 24°C; and get the coils and drain cleaned in winter, so the unit is not fighting last summer's dust all next summer. Curtains on west-facing glass in the afternoon help too — but the AC is where the money is.

If your bill jumped suddenly and the AC is not the cause, check the rest of the home first — a running toilet, a water heater left on, a faulty meter. For the rainy season, DEWA itself urges regular maintenance of electrical wiring: see DEWA's preventive-measures notice and its rainy-season safety guidance.

Is an annual maintenance contract worth it?

An annual maintenance contract in Dubai — an AMC — is one contract, scheduled visits through the year, and one number to call when something breaks. It suits a villa owner with five or six units or a landlord with several flats. A one-bedroom flat with a single split unit is usually better off booking a winter visit and a pre-summer visit as one-off jobs.

Before signing anything, ask three questions: what is covered — AC only, or plumbing and electrical too? How many scheduled visits a year, and what happens between them if something breaks? And what is excluded — parts, gas, duct cleaning? A contract that answers those clearly is worth considering. Al Hamra offers AC service and repair, electrical, plumbing and general maintenance across Dubai, so a contract conversation starts with what your home actually has.

When winter maintenance is not enough — and when to call a plumber

Winter maintenance is planned work. Some things are not planned, and pretending otherwise would be dishonest. If water is coming through a ceiling, if a pipe has burst, if a toilet will not stop running, or if the water heater has given up on a cold morning — that is not a November checklist item. That is a call now, and the longer you wait, the bigger the bill.

That is where the rest of the team comes in. An emergency plumber in Dubai is the person you ring when water is where it should not be. Frequently asked questions

Is winter really the best time for AC maintenance in Dubai?

Yes. November to February is the recognised winter maintenance window. The AC has just finished a long summer, technicians are not booked solid, and a service now means the unit is clean and checked before the heat returns in March and April.

What does a winter AC maintenance visit cover?

Filter cleaning or replacement, coil cleaning, drain clearing and testing, outdoor unit checks, refrigerant and electrical checks, thermostat testing, and a full run to confirm it cools properly.

How much does AC maintenance cost in Dubai?

Public 2026 Dubai guides put a split AC service at roughly AED 100–300 per unit and a deep clean at roughly AED 200–350 per unit. The final price depends on the number of units, their condition and access, and is confirmed after inspection.

Does AC maintenance lower my DEWA bill?

It can help — that is the practical answer to how to reduce DEWA bill in Dubai. A dirty filter or fouled coil makes the compressor run longer and use more electricity. Cleaning the filters and coils, and keeping the thermostat at 24°C as DEWA advises, are the two simplest ways to keep cooling costs down.

How often should an AC be serviced in Dubai?

Most Dubai guides recommend every three to six months, and at least once a year before summer. Homes near the coast, with pets, or with heavy daily use usually need the shorter interval.

Can Al Hamra handle other jobs in the same visit?

Often, yes. Mention everything when you book — an AC service plus a leaking tap plus a sticking door — and the right specialists and materials can come together in one visit.
