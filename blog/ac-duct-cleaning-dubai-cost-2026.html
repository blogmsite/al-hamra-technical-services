1. The 6 signs your ducts need cleaning

These are the signs the Dubai guides agree on. One of them on its own is worth an inspection; two or more together mean the ducts are due.

1. A musty smell from the vents — especially after the unit itself was cleaned. This is the tell. If the indoor unit has been serviced and the smell is still there, the smell is coming from inside the ductwork, not the unit. Dust and humidity in a ducted system can develop mould that circulates through every room connected to it.

2. Dust around the vents, or dust blowing from them. Visible dust at the grilles, or a puff of dust when the AC starts, means material has built up inside the duct and is now moving.

3. Uneven cooling. One room never feels quite cool enough while the others do. A partially blocked duct run starves that room of air.

4. Rooms that never feel quite cool, or cooling that has got slower. The system is working harder for the same result — the same symptom that pushes a DEWA bill up, which is why dirty ducts belong in the same conversation as the four checks in our guide to why your DEWA bill is so high.

5. Allergies or irritation indoors that ease when you leave the house. Dust, pollen and mould spores circulating through the ducts are a common trigger in Dubai homes, and a duct clean is the fix that addresses the source rather than the symptom.

6. Visible dust or mould at the grilles. If you can see it at the grille, there is more of it further in where you cannot see.

A note on what is NOT a duct problem: a unit that leaks water onto the floor is usually a blocked AC drain line, not a duct fault — our guide to blocked drains in Dubai covers the drain side, and the drain line is cleaned in the same visit as a duct clean.

2. What a proper duct clean actually covers

Duct cleaning is a distinct service from a routine unit service, and the scope is where quotes differ. A proper ducted AC cleaning Dubai job should include:

  1. Inspection first. The technician inspects the system and gives a written quotation before starting work. A company that quotes a price without looking at the ducts is guessing.
  2. Brush and vacuum through the duct run. Powered agitation loosens material from the duct walls; a vacuum — ideally a HEPA-filtered extraction system, often truck-mounted — pulls it out of the whole duct length. This is the core of the job.
  3. Grilles and filters. Grilles are removed and cleaned; filters are washed or replaced.
  4. Evaporator coil and drain pan. The coil and the drain pan sit at the start of the duct run. A clean that skips them leaves the dirtiest part untouched — and a blocked drain pan is what leaks water through a ceiling.
  5. Sanitising. An antimicrobial fogging of the duct interior after cleaning, for systems that have had mould or a musty smell.
  6. A camera report. A certified full service includes a camera inspection of the duct interior, before and after, so you can see what was actually done.

What a duct clean is NOT: it is not a filter wash, it is not a coil clean on its own, and it is not a gas top-up. If a quote under AED 100 per unit is offered, ask what is actually included — the published guides are clear that the cheapest offers in this category are the ones that skip the coil, the drain and the camera.

A word on certification: the best-known standard in this trade is NADCA (the National Air Duct Cleaners Association). A certified job follows its method — negative pressure, camera report, sanitising. This guide describes what a certified job looks like; it does not claim Al Hamra holds NADCA certification, because no certification has been verified. Ask any company what standard it works to, and what its camera report shows.

Three things in that table deserve a closer look.

The price is quoted three different ways, which is why comparison is so difficult. Some companies charge per AC unit, some per bedroom, some a flat rate for the property, some per outlet or grille, and some per linear metre of duct. Before you compare two quotes, convert them to the same basis — per unit, with the coil, drain and sanitising listed separately — or you are comparing different jobs.

The scope explains the spread. Brush and vacuum alone is the cheap end (AED 150–280 per unit). Add the evaporator coil and you are at AED 250–400. The full certified job with negative pressure and a camera report tops out around AED 550 per unit. A quote that is far below the band for its scope is a sign to walk away, not a bargain.

The starting price is the floor, not the total. One Dubai provider lists duct cleaning from AED 300 per unit, with the final price depending on the number of units and the service hours. A studio with one unit starts at AED 300; a villa with four units starts at AED 1,200 — before the coil, the sanitising or any access work is added. Insist on the inspection first, and on a written quote that itemises what is included.

4. Duct clean vs AC service vs AC repair — which symptom needs which

Residents book the wrong one of these three jobs more often than any other mistake in AC maintenance. Here is the split, in one paragraph each.

AC service is the routine visit: filters, coil cleaning on both sides, drain-line flush and pan clean, refrigerant pressure check, electrical health check, thermostat calibration. It is the job our guide to winter AC maintenance in Dubai describes, and it is what a split unit in an apartment usually needs. Book it every year, in the November-to-February window.

AC duct cleaning is the job for ducted systems — villas, townhouses, penthouses and larger apartments with ceiling-concealed units. It deals with the passages that carry air to the rooms. Book it when the signs in section 1 show up, or every 12–18 months.

AC repair is the job for a fault: a fan that will not spin, a compressor that will not start, a unit that trips the breaker, a refrigerant leak. A grinding or screeching noise suggests fan-motor bearings are failing; bubbling or hissing can indicate a refrigerant leak; continuous clicking suggests a control-board issue. Repair is priced per fault after diagnosis — a diagnostic visit is commonly AED 100–250, and a fan-motor replacement AED 350–700. If a company quotes a compressor replacement without first doing a diagnostic check, walk away.

The rule of thumb: if the unit itself is the problem, it is a service or a repair. If the air coming out of the vents is the problem — smell, dust, uneven cooling — it is the ducts.

5. How often should ducts be cleaned?

The published interval depends on who you ask, and the honest answer is that it depends on the home:

  • Every 12–18 months for most ducted systems under normal Dubai conditions (Casttio).
  • Every 1–2 years for villas with central ducted systems (Clean Living UAE).
  • Every 2–3 years in some guides, for systems that have been cleaned regularly and have no mould history (European Technical).

A system that has not been cleaned since it was installed, or since a renovation, is overdue — renovation dust settles in ducts and stays there. November is the natural booking moment: the dusty summer is over, the cooling load has dropped, and the job can be booked alongside the winter AC service in a single visit.

6. When the ducts are above a false ceiling

In most Dubai villas and many apartments, the ducts run above a false ceiling — a gypsum board ceiling with a void behind it. That changes the job in three ways.

Access. Every fan coil unit, valve, damper and junction box behind a ceiling needs a properly positioned access hatch. If there is no hatch, the ceiling has to be opened — and that is a gypsum job, not just an AC job. A ceiling access panel Dubai search is how residents find the hatch itself; the panel should be planned into the design so servicing never damages the finish.

Damage. A sagging false ceiling Dubai is the symptom residents actually type into Google — and it is a safety fault, not a cosmetic one. A board that sags away from the frame, or paint that bubbles, means the board has absorbed water and lost its integrity. The cause is usually a leak above: a pipe, an AC drain, or a bathroom. The published 2026 bands are: minor crack or hole repair from AED 149, water damage treatment from AED 399, sagging ceiling support from AED 449 (Fix On Click, updated 20 days ago); hairline crack fill and repaint AED 200–500 per room, small water stain AED 300–600, medium section replacement AED 500–1,200, large section AED 1,000–2,500, full bedroom ceiling AED 1,500–3,500 (Orial, 1 Jul 2026); a standard false-ceiling job from AED 499 (Top Fixers JLT, updated 1 day ago).

The rule that cannot be skipped. Never repair a gypsum ceiling without first fixing the source of any water leak. Patching over a wet ceiling is a waste of money — the damage returns within weeks. Water-damaged gypsum must dry completely before repair, usually 48–72 hours with good ventilation. A sagging or water-stained board must be replaced, not painted over — painting over a stain without finding the cause brings it back. And a false ceiling water damage Dubai repair that skips the leak check is not a repair at all. 7. The repaint that finishes the job — interior painting cost per sqm Dubai

Once ceiling panels have been opened, refitted or replaced, the room usually needs repainting — and this is where residents overpay, because painting is quoted per square metre of painted surface (walls and ceiling combined), not per square metre of floor area.

The interior painting cost per sqm Dubai bands for 2026 are public and consistent: standard emulsion AED 15–22/m², washable eggshell or silk AED 20–28/m² (Vision Three, 20 Jul 2026); standard repaint AED 12–30/m², premium finish or colour change AED 28–46/m² (SKN, updated 13 Oct 2026). A full villa repaint, inside and out, runs AED 18,000–45,000+ — that is a project, not a call-out.

8. What to ask before you book

Ask these six questions, in writing, before any duct-cleaning company starts work:

  1. Does the quote include the coil and the drain pan, or just the ducts? The coil and drain are the dirtiest part of the run.
  2. Is sanitising included, and what product is used? It matters most when there has been mould or a musty smell.
  3. Do I get a camera report, before and after? If there is no camera, there is no proof the ducts were actually cleaned.
  4. Is the price per unit, per duct, per grille, or per property — and how many of each do I have? Convert every quote to the same basis before comparing.
  5. Is there an access hatch, or will the ceiling be opened? If the ceiling is opened, who repairs and repaints it, and is that in the quote?
  6. What is the warranty on the work? A quality repair should carry at least a 30-day warranty on the work; 90 days on parts like motors is reasonable.

And one more, for the ceiling: was the leak above fixed first, and has the board dried for 48–72 hours? If the answer is no, the job is not ready.

A duct clean is not a filter wash and it is not a gas top-up. It is the job for the passages that carry cooled air to every room — and in a Dubai home, five months of desert dust, sand and humidity settle in those passages every summer. In November, with the cooling load down and the winter service window open, one visit can cover the ducts, the unit, the drain and the ceiling access in a single booking. Get the inspection first, get the quote in writing, and check that the coil, the drain and the camera report are in it.

Frequently asked questions

How much does duct cleaning cost in Dubai in 2026?

The duct cleaning Dubai cost bands for 2026 are: for a studio or 1-bedroom apartment, AED 300–650; for a 2–3-bedroom apartment, AED 550–1,200; for a 3-bedroom villa, AED 800–2,500. Per unit, a standard clean is AED 150–280, duct + coil AED 250–400, and a certified full service with negative pressure and a camera report AED 400–550. These are public 2026 market bands from named Dubai sources, not Al Hamra's prices — a firm price always follows an inspection.

How do I know if my AC ducts need cleaning?

The six signs are: a musty smell from the vents (especially after the unit was cleaned), dust around or blowing from the vents, uneven cooling between rooms, slower cooling, allergies indoors that ease when you leave, and visible dust or mould at the grilles. Two or more together mean the ducts are due.

Is duct cleaning the same as AC servicing?

No. AC servicing is the routine visit for the unit itself — filters, coil, drain, refrigerant, electrics. Duct cleaning is the job for the passages that carry air to the rooms, in ducted systems. A split unit in an apartment usually needs a service; a ducted villa system needs both, on different intervals.

How often should AC ducts be cleaned in Dubai?

Every 12–18 months for most ducted systems, or every 1–2 years for villas with central ducted systems. A system that has not been cleaned since installation, or since a renovation, is overdue. November is the natural booking moment, alongside the winter AC service.

My false ceiling is sagging — is that urgent?

Yes. A sagging false ceiling Dubai search usually means a board that has absorbed water and lost its integrity. Support the ceiling, find and fix the leak above first, let the board dry for 48–72 hours, then replace the damaged section. Painting over it brings the damage back within weeks.

What does interior painting cost per square metre in Dubai?

Standard emulsion AED 15–22/m² and washable eggshell or silk AED 20–28/m² of painted surface (walls + ceiling, not floor area), two coats and standard preparation included. A 1-bedroom apartment runs AED 1,200–1,800 and a 2–3-bedroom apartment AED 1,800–2,400. These are public 2026 market bands from named Dubai sources.
