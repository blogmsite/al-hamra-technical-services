Privacy Policy

What we collect, why we collect it, and what happens to it — in plain language.

Last updated: 10 November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information Al Hamra Technical Services Co. ("Al Hamra", "we") collects when you use this website, how it is used, and the choices you have. This website does not ask you to create an account, and there is no login on this site.

1. Information you send us

When you contact us, you send us the details you choose to include. That is usually your name, your phone or WhatsApp number, the service you need, your area in Dubai, a preferred date and time, and a description of the problem.

You send those details in one of these ways:

  • WhatsApp — by messaging us directly, or through the booking form on the homepage, which opens WhatsApp with your request already written out for you to send.
  • Email — by emailing us directly, or through the booking form, which opens your email app with a draft addressed to us.
  • Phone — by calling either of the numbers on the Contact page.

The booking form itself does not store or send your details anywhere on this website. Your message only goes where you send it — to WhatsApp or to your email app, when you press send there.

2. How we use that information

We use the details you send only to reply to you, to understand the job, to arrange a visit, and to give you a price after a technician has looked at the job. We do not sell your contact details, and we do not use them to send marketing messages you have not asked for.

3. Website analytics — Cloudflare Web Analytics

This site uses Cloudflare Web Analytics to count visits and see, in general terms, which pages are visited. It counts visits without personal tracking and without accounts: it does not identify individual visitors, and it does not require you to sign in or be tracked across sites. Analytics figures are held by Cloudflare under its own terms.

4. Cookies

This site does not use accounts or logins, and it does not set cookies to remember who you are. Some of the third-party services listed below may set their own cookies when their content or links are used — for example, if advertising is added in future (see section 5). Your browser settings let you block or delete cookies; blocking them will not stop you using this site.

5. Third-party services

This site links to, or loads content from, these third-party services. Each has its own privacy policy, and your use of that service is covered by that policy, not this one:

  • WhatsApp — for messages and booking requests sent through WhatsApp.
  • Email — for messages sent to our email address.
  • Cloudflare Web Analytics — for counting visits, as described in section 3.
  • Instagram — this site links to our Instagram profile, @alhmratechnicalservices.
  • Google Fonts — the fonts on this site are loaded from Google Fonts.

6. Third-party advertising — preparing for ads

This site does not currently show third-party ads. We are preparing the site for advertising, which may include Google AdSense in future.

If advertising is added, third-party vendors, including Google, may use cookies to serve ads based on your visits to this site or to other sites on the internet. You can opt out of personalised advertising from Google by visiting Google Ads Settings at https://adssettings.google.com. You can also find out more about how Google uses cookies in advertising at https://www.google.com/policies/technologies/ads/.

We will update this page if and when advertising goes live on this site.

7. How long we keep information

We keep the messages you send us only for as long as needed to deal with your enquiry or booking. We do not keep a separate customer database on this website, and we do not publish your details anywhere on this site.

8. Your choices

You do not have to send us any details to read this website. If you have sent us a message and want to ask about it, contact us using the details below and tell us what you sent and when.

9. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
